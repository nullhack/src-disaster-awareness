#### Tropical Cyclone ONE-26 India
*LOW · 17 articles · India*

> Selected reports track the Bay of Bengal deep depression as it approached and crossed the Andhra-Odisha coast near Kalingapatnam, with IMD warning of extremely heavy rain and winds up to 75 km/hr.
