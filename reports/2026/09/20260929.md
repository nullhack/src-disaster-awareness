#### Tropical Cyclone ONE-26 India
*LOW · 17 articles · India*

> Selected reports track the Bay of Bengal deep depression as it approached and crossed the Andhra-Odisha coast near Kalingapatnam, with IMD warning of extremely heavy rain and winds up to 75 km/hr. Red alerts were issued for districts in Andhra Pradesh and Odisha, and schools were closed in parts of Odisha. ### Asia

#### Tropical Cyclone ONE-26 India [NEW]
*LOW · 9 articles · India*

> A weather system over the Bay of Bengal is set to make landfall between Andhra Pradesh and Odisha on September 22, 2026, with schools and liquor shops closed in Malkangiri. The Joint Typhoon Warning Center classifies it as Tropical Cyclone 01B, while the India Meteorological Department officially considers it a deep depression; social media erroneously named it "Cyclone Arnab". Red alerts and heavy rain warnings are in place for the affected areas. The deep depression that formed over the Bay of Bengal has made landfall along the Andhra-Odisha coast. Over 1,500 people were evacuated from Gajapati district, and red alerts remain in effect as heavy rain continues.
